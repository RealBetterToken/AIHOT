Page introduction:

> You do not need to register or sign in to use this site. We process only the information needed to operate the site and handle feedback, and do not sell personal information.

## 1. Data stored in your browser

Bookmarks, read history, topic preferences and feedback drafts are saved only in your current browser and are not sent to the server. Clearing browser data removes them; they do not sync across devices. You can export and import these data on the “Bookmarks” page.

## 2. Feedback

The feedback you submit, your optional email address, the page address at submission and any screenshot you choose to attach are stored on the server to handle your feedback. To prevent abuse, the server stores an irreversible identifier derived from the network address and browser category, used for rate limiting and blocking abusive sources.

## 3. Server and network logs

This software does not collect visitor analytics by default. Your server, reverse proxy or CDN may record access logs according to their own settings (such as IP address, access time, page address and browser information). State here the services you actually use and their retention periods.

## 4. Third-party content

This site displays summaries of and links to third-party original articles. Following an original link takes you to the other website, where its privacy policy applies.
